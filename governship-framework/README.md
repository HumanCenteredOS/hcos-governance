## Storyteller Governship

Governship must preserve not only evidence, decisions, controls, and outcomes, but also the human meaning and lived experience that systems can lose.

The HCOS™ Storyteller Governship Lens helps reviewers:

- preserve chronology and context
- distinguish facts, accounts, interpretations, and synthesis
- map differing stakeholder perspectives
- identify missing or silenced voices
- protect consent, privacy, dignity, and correction rights
- connect human experience with system conditions
- preserve lessons for future stewardship

- [HCOS™ Storyteller Governship Lens](instruments/HCOS_Storyteller_Governship_Lens.md)

> **The storyteller does not replace evidence. The storyteller protects the meaning that evidence alone may not preserve.**
