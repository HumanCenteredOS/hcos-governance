# HCOS™ Governship Maturity Assessment

## Purpose

This assessment supports reflection on the maturity of governance and stewardship practices.

## Scoring Scale

1 — Rarely true  
2 — Sometimes true  
3 — Usually true  
4 — Consistently true  
5 — Embedded and continuously improved

## Domain 1 — Human Purpose

- Decisions are connected to a clear human purpose.
- Organizational priorities remain aligned with mission.
- Tradeoffs affecting people are openly considered.
