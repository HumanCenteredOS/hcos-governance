## Instructions

Use this worksheet to examine a concern without prematurely deciding whether the complete interpretation is true or false.

Record observations separately from conclusions.

The purpose is:

**Understanding + Protection + Evidence Growth + Responsible Stewardship**

—not winning an argument.
